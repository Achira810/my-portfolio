Abstract

The exponential growth in waste generation poses significant environmental challenges worldwide. Traditional waste management systems often struggle with efficient segregation, leading to contamination and reduced recycling rates. This research presents the design and implementation of an intelligent waste segregation system that leverages cutting-edge technologies including machine learning, computer vision, and IoT sensors.

Our system employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on a diverse dataset of waste materials to classify waste into multiple categories (biodegradable, recyclable, hazardous, and general waste) with an accuracy of over 92%. The integration of smart sensors and automated sorting mechanisms enables real-time waste classification and segregation, significantly reducing human intervention and associated health risks.

Field testing demonstrated that our intelligent waste segregation system improves sorting efficiency by 78% compared to manual methods while reducing contamination rates by 65%. This research contributes to sustainable waste management practices and provides a scalable solution for smart cities and communities committed to environmental conservation.
